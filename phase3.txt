Phase 3 — Help-request form (/ask-for-help)

Context: Read PRD.md (design system, tone, legal limits). Builds on Phase 2 (next-intl he/en, theme tokens).

Flow: one question per screen, gentle progress indicator, Back on every screen,
calm Exit on every screen (asks "are you sure?", returns home, nothing saved).

1. What kind of support? (multi-select, icon cards with one-line descriptions)
   - Document organization & preparation (sorting mail, downloading medical summaries
     from the provider portal, photographing/scanning/compressing files for upload)
   - Rights mapping & plain-language explanation (review possible benefits, translate jargon,
     build a checklist of required documents)
   - Co-filling online forms ("co-typing": volunteer reads and types what the applicant
     dictates; the applicant logs in and clicks Submit themselves)
   - Scheduling appointments & evaluations
   - Support during helpline calls (applicant dials and verifies identity, then speakerphone)
   - In-person accompaniment to medical committees & moral support
   - Gentle reminders & ongoing follow-up
2. How would you like to meet? In person / Video / Phone / Whatever works
   2b. If in person: city or area
3. Preferred language: Hebrew, Arabic, Russian, English
4. Optional short note for the volunteer (warn: no medical details)
5. Contact details: first name, last name, phone (required, Israeli format), email (optional, recommended)
   NO national ID number.
6. Scope agreement checkbox ("I understand volunteers support me but don't act on my behalf"), link to /terms. Send.
7. Confirmation: "We received your request. A volunteer will call you."

Data: Supabase.
- help_requests: support types, meeting mode, city, language, note, first name, status, locale.
- help_request_contacts: last name, phone, email (kept apart so volunteers see them only after accepting).
- Visitors can only submit, via a single database function; no one can read requests without the secret key.
- No file uploads anywhere.

Out of scope: optional help-seeker account and "my request" status page (next phase), volunteer side, auto-save.
